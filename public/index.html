The problem

Global events don't have a venue problem. They have a visa problem they discover after the venue is booked. The people refused at the border never show up in the attendance numbers.

Graph native

Whether someone can attend is a chain, not a lookup. Hostable stores entry rules, refusal rates, visa unlocks and flight routes as a FalkorDB graph and walks every nationality to every city in one traversal per city.

Honest numbers

Keeps counts only people who can be shown to get in. Lost is people needing a visa times the published refusal rate. Where no rate is published, they are unknown, never counted as kept.
